# Marketing Plan

Make fans, not headlines. Teach, don't pitch.

## When to use

You have paying customers and want to grow. Or you're planning how to reach your community.

## The principle

Minimalist marketing is content marketing done honestly. You teach what you know, share what you build, help people for free, and the product sells itself to the people who need it. No growth hacks. No paid acquisition. No viral loops. Just being genuinely useful in public.

## How to apply

1. **Teach what you know.** Write about the problem you're solving. Not "why our product is great" — "here's how to solve X" (and by the way, we built a tool that does it).

2. **Build in public.** Share your revenue, your decisions, your mistakes. People root for transparent founders.

3. **Pick one channel and go deep:**
   - Where does your community already hang out?
   - Go there. Be helpful. Don't pitch.
   - One channel done well beats five done poorly.

4. **Create a flywheel:**
   - Help someone → they tell someone → that person finds your content → they become a customer → they tell someone
   - Every piece of content should serve the flywheel

5. **Measure what matters:** Customers, not followers. Revenue, not impressions. Retention, not signups.

## Output

- Your one primary channel and why
- Content plan: 4 pieces of content that teach something real
- Distribution plan: how each piece reaches your community
- Metrics: what you'll measure and what you'll ignore

## Anti-patterns

- Spending money on ads before product-market fit
- Optimizing for vanity metrics (followers, likes, impressions)
- Hiring a marketing person before you can do the marketing yourself
- "Launch day" as a strategy instead of continuous presence
