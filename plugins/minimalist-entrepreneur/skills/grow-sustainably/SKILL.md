# Grow Sustainably

Spend less than you make. That's it. That's the whole thing.

## When to use

You have revenue and want to grow without burning money or raising venture capital.

## The principle

A minimalist business is profitable from early on. Growth comes from reinvesting revenue, not from burning investor money. Hire slow. Automate what you can. Keep costs below revenue. The business should fund itself.

## How to apply

1. **Know your numbers.** Revenue, costs, profit margin, burn rate, runway. Update monthly. If you can't state these from memory, fix that first.

2. **Automate before hiring.** Can an AI agent do it? Can a Zapier workflow do it? Can a script do it? Only hire a human when automation genuinely can't handle it.

3. **Hire contractors before employees.** Test the role with a contractor first. If it works, consider making it full-time. If it doesn't, you haven't committed to a salary.

4. **Set a growth budget.** X% of last month's revenue goes to growth. No more. If revenue drops, spending drops automatically.

5. **Profitability milestones:**
   - $1K MRR: covers basic tools and services
   - $5K MRR: covers one person's living expenses
   - $10K MRR: can hire first contractor
   - $50K MRR: can hire first employee
   - $100K MRR: real business with real margin

## Output

- Current financial snapshot (revenue, costs, margin, runway)
- What's automated vs. manual vs. needs a hire
- Growth budget calculation
- Next profitability milestone and plan to reach it

## Anti-patterns

- Raising money when you could be profitable
- Hiring ahead of revenue
- Spending on office/perks/tools before product-market fit
- Growing users without growing revenue
