# First Customers

Sell to 100 people. By hand. One at a time.

## When to use

You have an MVP. Now get it into people's hands and get them to pay.

## The principle

Your first 100 customers don't come from marketing. They come from you personally reaching out, one by one, to people in your community. This doesn't scale — that's the point. You learn from every conversation.

## How to apply

1. **Start with your pre-sale customers.** They already said yes. Deliver, follow up, ask for feedback.

2. **Ask each happy customer for one introduction.** "Who else do you know who has this problem?" One warm intro is worth 100 cold emails.

3. **Go where your community hangs out.** Post in the forum/Discord/Slack. Not "buy my thing" — share what you built and why. Show the work. Be helpful first.

4. **Do things that don't scale:**
   - Onboard every customer personally
   - Send handwritten thank-you notes
   - Fix problems in real-time over DM
   - Customize the product for individual needs

5. **Track everything.** Where each customer came from, what convinced them, what almost lost them. This becomes your playbook.

## Output

- A customer tracker: who, when, how they found you, what they paid, their feedback
- Your conversion funnel: how many conversations → how many trials → how many paid
- The top 3 reasons people buy, and the top 3 reasons they don't

## Anti-patterns

- Running ads before you have 100 customers
- Building a self-serve onboarding before you've onboarded 50 people manually
- Hiring a salesperson before you can sell it yourself
- Discounting to get the first customers (charge full price from day 1)
